Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Last updated: October 2026 · Claim4u ("Your Housing Claims Partner") · UK GDPR Compliant

At Claim4u, we take the confidentiality, privacy, and statutory protections of tenants with utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, and process your personal information when you use our website, contact us via phone (07473956657), or message us via WhatsApp (07473956657).

1. Who We Are & Regulatory Status

Claim4u operates as a dedicated UK legal claims intake and marketing service connecting council and housing association tenants suffering from housing disrepair with authorized, SRA-regulated solicitor partners across England and Wales. We are committed to ethical marketing and absolute transparency.

2. Personal Information We Collect

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, telephone contact number, email address, and UK postal code.
  • Tenancy Information: Landlord name (Local Authority Council or Housing Association) and duration of disrepair.
  • Disrepair Details: Nature of defects (damp and toxic mould, water leaks, heating/boiler failures, structural cracks, pest infestations), personal descriptions, and photos you choose to provide.
  • Digital Attribution & Advertising Analytics: UTM campaign identifiers, Google Click IDs (gclid), Meta Click IDs (fbclid), and anonymous device metadata to assess ad performance.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (UK GDPR & DPA 2018)

We process your personal information in accordance with Article 6 of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR):

  • Consent: Explicitly granted when you submit our 60-second eligibility form or initiate communication on WhatsApp.
  • Legitimate Interests: To review, evaluate, and refer your claim to an independent SRA-regulated housing solicitor for formal legal representation.

4. Confidentiality & Anti-Retaliation Protections

We will never contact your landlord without your explicit instruction through your appointed solicitor. Council and housing association tenants in the UK are legally protected against retaliatory evictions under the Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018 and Landlord and Tenant Act 1985. Making a legitimate disrepair claim is your legal right.

5. Advertising Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use privacy-conscious conversion tags, including Google Ads (Google Tag) and Meta Pixel (Facebook Ads), to gauge campaign efficiency and measure inquiry completions. These cookies do not store unencrypted personal information. You can opt out of targeted advertising via your browser settings or Your Online Choices.

6. Your Individual Statutory Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request a copy of your personal information, request rectification of inaccuracies, or request the immediate deletion of your data from our systems at any time.
