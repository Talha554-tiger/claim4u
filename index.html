FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Key Questions

Which areas of law and locations do you cover? +

We assist with Housing Disrepair (for council and housing association tenants) and Road Traffic Accidents (for non-fault drivers, passengers, pedestrians and cyclists). Our panel strictly operates across England and Wales only. We do not take claims in Scotland or Northern Ireland.

How does No Win, No Fee work? +

If your claim proceeds with our SRA-regulated solicitor partners, it is handled under a Conditional Fee Agreement (CFA). You pay strictly £0 upfront. If the claim is unsuccessful, you pay nothing. If successful, solicitor fees are capped and deducted from the compensation payout.

Can my council landlord evict me for claiming? +

No. Tenants of councils and housing associations in England and Wales are legally protected against retaliatory evictions under the Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018. It is your statutory right to live in a safe home.
