How Much Compensation for UK Housing Disrepair?

Most successful UK housing disrepair claims settle between £1,500 and £15,000+. What you receive depends on the severity of the disrepair, how long your landlord ignored it, health ill effects, and damage to your personal belongings.

Payout Bands

Typical Compensation by Type of Disrepair

Claims often involve more than one issue — damp caused by a leak, or pests entering through structural cracks — and those combine into a single, larger award.

💧 Damp & Toxic Black Mould

£1,500 – £12,000+

Calculated based on percentage of rent refunded (25%–50%) for the duration, plus additional awards under Awaab's Law for asthma, bronchitis, or respiratory illness caused by mould spores.

🧱 Structural Cracks & Foundation Movement

£2,000 – £15,000+

Cracking walls, collapsing ceilings, sagging floors, staircase hazards, and external brickwork decay where family safety was compromised under Section 11 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985.

🔥 Broken Boilers & Heating Failure

£1,500 – £7,500+

Freezing living conditions, broken radiators, lack of hot water, and excessive electricity bills from using temporary plug-in fan heaters.

🚰 Internal Leaks & Water Ingress

£1,800 – £10,000+

Unresolved roof leaks, dripping ceilings, burst pipes, and water intrusion causing ruined carpets, furniture, and hazardous electrical faults.

🐀 Infestations (Rats, Mice, Pests)

£1,500 – £8,500+

Pest entry due to unsealed council pipe cavities, defective drainage, or untreated structural decay causing severe psychological distress and unsanitary conditions.

🚪 Rotten Windows & Insecure Doors

£1,200 – £6,000+

Rotten wooden frames, broken locking mechanisms, excessive draughts, and water pouring through failed seals, resulting in lost thermal insulation.

Valuation Factors

Five Things That Decide What Your Claim Is Worth

Under UK Civil Procedure Rules and Judicial College guidelines, housing disrepair compensation comprises three primary pillars:

FACTOR 01

How Long It Went Unrepaired

Compensation is calculated in part as a percentage of the rent you paid — commonly 25% to 50% of rent for the period in disrepair. Longer means higher payouts.

FACTOR 02

Health Impacts

Asthma, chest infections, skin conditions, anxiety and sleep loss significantly increase the general damages element, supported by medical or GP notes.

FACTOR 03

Financial Losses

Ruined furniture, sofas, clothing, electrical appliances, increased heating bills, and dehumidifier running costs are claimed back in full as special damages.

FACTOR 04

Rooms Affected

A single hallway patch is treated differently to a family bedroom or living room rendered completely unusable for months on end.

FACTOR 05

Evidence & Complaints

Dated photos, council repair reference numbers, emails, and complaint correspondence prove landlord negligence and maximize settlement speed.
