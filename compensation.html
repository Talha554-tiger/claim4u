How Much Compensation for UK Housing Disrepair?

Most successful UK housing disrepair claims settle between £1,500 and £15,000. What you receive depends on the severity of the disrepair, how long your landlord ignored it, and what it cost you in health, comfort and belongings.

Payout Bands

Typical Compensation by Type of Disrepair

Claims often involve more than one issue — damp caused by a leak, or pests entering through structural cracks — and those combine into a single, larger award.

💧 Damp & Toxic Black Mould

£1,500 – £12,000+

Calculated based on percentage of rent refunded (25%–50%) for the duration, plus additional awards for asthma or respiratory illness caused by spores.

🧱 Structural Cracks & Shifting

£2,000 – £15,000+

Cracking walls, collapsing ceilings, sagging floors, and stair structural hazards where family safety was compromised.

🐀 Infestations (Rats, Mice, Pests)

£1,500 – £8,500+

Pest entry due to unsealed council cavities, defective pipes, or untreated structural decay causing distress and unsanitary conditions.

🚰 Internal Leaks & Water Ingress

£1,800 – £10,000+

Unresolved roof leaks, dripping ceilings, burst pipes, and water intrusion causing ruined carpets, furniture, and electrical hazards.

Valuation Factors

Five Things That Decide What Your Claim Is Worth

Compensation is made up of general damages (discomfort and ill health), a rent-based element (rent abatement), and special damages (out-of-pocket costs).

FACTOR 01

How Long It Went Unrepaired

Compensation is calculated in part as a percentage of the rent you paid — commonly 25% to 50% of rent for the period in disrepair. Longer means higher payouts.

FACTOR 02

Health Impacts

Asthma, chest infections, skin conditions, anxiety and sleep loss significantly increase the general damages element, supported by medical or GP notes.

FACTOR 03

Financial Losses

Ruined furniture, sofas, clothing, electrical appliances, increased heating bills, and dehumidifier running costs are claimed back in full as special damages.

FACTOR 04

Rooms Affected

A single hallway patch is treated differently to a family bedroom or living room rendered completely unusable for months on end.

FACTOR 05

Evidence & Complaints

Dated photos, council repair reference numbers, emails, and complaint correspondence prove landlord negligence and maximize settlement speed.
