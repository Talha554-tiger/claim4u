How Much RTA Compensation in England & Wales?

Payouts consist of General Damages (for physical and psychological injury) plus Special Damages (for all vehicle write-offs, repairs, hire cars and loss of income).

Injury Category (England & Wales) Recovery Duration Compensation Payout Range
Minor Whiplash & Neck Strain Up to 3 months £240 – £275 (tariff)
Whiplash with Psychological Anxiety 6 to 12 months £1,430 – £2,160
Severe Whiplash / 24-Month Impairment 18 to 24 months £4,215 – £4,345
Moderate Neck, Back & Spine Trauma (Non-Tariff) Permanent / Ongoing £13,740 – £38,490
Wrist, Arm or Collarbone Fractures Surgical pinning / cast £6,610 – £34,340
Severe Head Trauma & Chronic Brain Injury High impact collisions £43,060 – £403,990
