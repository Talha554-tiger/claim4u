STATUTORY OIC TARIFF BANDS

Civil Liability Act 2018 Statutory Tariffs

Under England & Wales law, minor whiplash injuries resulting from road accidents are assessed according to the official fixed statutory tariff:

Duration of Whiplash Whiplash Only Whiplash + Psychological Symptoms
Not exceeding 3 months £240 £275
3 to 6 months £495 £520
6 to 9 months £840 £895
9 to 12 months £1,320 £1,390
12 to 15 months £2,040 £2,120
15 to 18 months £3,005 £3,070
18 to 24 months £4,215 £4,345

💡 Why Serious Non-Whiplash Injuries Receive Significantly Higher Compensation

The tariffs above strictly apply to soft tissue neck/back sprains. If your accident also involved fractures, shoulder tears, knee ligament damage, chronic disc trauma, or severe concussion, your claim falls outside the restrictive tariff and is evaluated under the much higher Judicial College Guidelines (often £8,000 to £50,000+).

Roundabout Shunt in Newport, Wales

£14,200 Total Payout
